We need to pass fundraiser in contribute instruction because it requires fundraiser.maker as seed and maker lives as on-chain data inside fundraiser account state so since maker isn't available client-side, we can't derive it automatically in codama and needs to be passed. Similarly, vault seeds also requires on-chain state fundraiser.mint_to_raise and can't be derived on client without fetching it first. on the other hand both contributor and contributor_ata seeds doesn't depend on any on-chain state and can completely be derived from the other accounts and off-chain info.
